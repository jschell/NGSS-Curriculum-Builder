## 🎯 Comparison with Other Jurisdictions

### DC's Unique Position

As a federal district rather than a state, DC has some unique characteristics:

**Advantages:**
- Single school district to coordinate (DCPS + charter schools)
- Smaller scale allows for more coordinated implementation
- Direct oversight by OSSE (Office of the State Superintendent of Education)
- Strong university partnerships (Georgetown, Howard, American, etc.)

**Challenges:**
- Urban setting with diverse student population
- Higher percentage of students requiring additional support
- Resource constraints in some schools
- Complex governance (local + federal oversight)
