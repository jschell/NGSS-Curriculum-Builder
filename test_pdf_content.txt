Grade K Science Standards
==========================

Kindergarten students should understand:
- Motion and stability
- Energy
- Waves and their applications
- Matter and its interactions

--- End of Kindergarten ---

Grade 1 Science Standards
==========================

First grade students should understand:
- Structure and properties of matter
- Chemical reactions
- Forces and interactions
- Energy and energy transfer

--- End of Grade 1 ---

Grade 2 Science Standards
==========================

Second grade students should understand:
- Interdependent relationships in ecosystems
- Cycles of matter and energy
- Structure, function, and information processing
- Evidence of common ancestry and diversity

--- End of Grade 2 ---

Grade 3 Science Standards
==========================

Third grade students should understand:
- Motion and stability: Forces and interactions
- Energy: Conservation and transfer
- Life cycles and traits

--- End of Grade 3 ---

Grade 4 Science Standards
==========================

Fourth grade students should understand:
- Energy and matter
- Earth's systems
- Earth and human activity

--- End of Grade 4 ---

Grade 5 Science Standards
==========================

Fifth grade students should understand:
- Structure and properties of matter
- Matter and energy in organisms and ecosystems
- Earth and human activity on systems

--- End of Grade 5 ---
